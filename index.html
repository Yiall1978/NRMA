Who We Are

Protecting what matters across Australia and New Zealand.

Insurance Australia Group is a general insurance group with businesses serving customers across Australia and New Zealand.

Through a portfolio of trusted brands and insurance businesses, IAG helps customers protect their homes, vehicles, businesses and other things that matter.

Our Purpose

Insurance that supports people and communities.

Our work goes beyond policies and claims. We aim to help customers recover from setbacks and contribute to safer, stronger and more resilient communities.
